I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the [University of Illinois Chicago (UIC)](https://www.uic.edu/), where I work as a graduate research assistant advised by [Dr. Austin Mordahl](https://austinmordahl.com/). My research lies at the intersection of agentic AI and software engineering.

Before joining UIC, I received my M.Sc. in Computer Science from [Bowling Green State University (BGSU)](https://www.bgsu.edu/) with a 4.0/4.0 GPA, advised by [Dr. Abbas Heydarnoori](https://heydarnoori.github.io/). At BGSU, I co-developed [LinkAnchor](https://doi.org/10.1145/3808191) (FSE 2026), an autonomous LLM-based agent for software traceability. I also led an empirical study of test failures in AI-generated pull requests ([MSR 2026 Mining Challenge](https://doi.org/10.1145/3793302.3793606)) and fine-tuned open-source LLMs for automatic issue assignment.

In addition to my academic research, I have industry experience as a software engineer at prominent tech companies, including [Digikala](https://www.digikala.com/), the largest e-commerce platform in Iran.

I received my B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from Sharif University of Technology, where I wrote my undergraduate thesis on the robustness of deep neural networks against spurious correlations advised by [Dr. Mahdieh Soleymani](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=S1U0KlgAAAAJ&hl=en).
